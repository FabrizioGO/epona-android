# Delete your Epona account

This page explains how to request deletion of your **Epona** account and the data associated with it. Epona is developed by {{ site.legal_name }}.

## Option 1: Delete it in the App (fastest)

1. Open **Epona** and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Scroll to the **Account** section and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted immediately from our active systems.

## Option 2: Request deletion without the App

If you no longer have the App installed, email **{{ site.contact_email }}** from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request". Include the email or name you registered with. We'll verify your identity and delete your account within a reasonable time, and confirm by email once it's done.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

- your profile (name, email, avatar);
- your pets, and any lost/found alerts you posted, including their photos;
- sighting reports you filed on other people's alerts, including their photos;
- your push-notification token and notification history;
- your authentication credentials.

## What we may retain, and for how long

- Copies of the data above may persist briefly in **encrypted backups** until those backups age out (typically within **30 days**).
- We may keep a **de-identified** record that a content report existed (without linking it back to your identity) for up to **12 months**, for safety and moderation purposes, as described in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html#6-data-retention).
- We are not required to, and do not, retain any data beyond what's described here.
